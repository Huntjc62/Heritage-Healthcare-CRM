💡 Key Insights

  • Total enquiries increased by 14%.
  • Conversion rates exceeded the company target.
  • Cardiff remains the highest-performing office.
  • Recruitment enquiries continue to grow nationally.
  • Website enquiries account for almost half of all leads.

  • Marketing campaigns generated higher-quality enquiries.

🎯 Recommendations

  • Increase digital marketing investment in lower-performing territories.
  • Share Cardiff's enquiry handling process as best practice.
  • Review response times for Milton Keynes and Basildon.
  • Continue improving website conversion pathways.
  • Expand successful recruitment campaigns nationally.
  • Introduce monthly performance reviews with franchisees.
  • Monitor enquiry quality alongside enquiry volume.
  • Continue investing in SEO and Google Business Profile optimisation.

📝 Executive Commentary

Overall, the Heritage Healthcare franchise network continues to demonstrate strong enquiry growth and healthy conversion performance. National response times have improved, with several offices consistently exceeding key performance indicators. Continued focus on digital marketing, rapid enquiry handling and sharing operational best practice between franchisees should support sustained growth throughout the remainder of the reporting year.
