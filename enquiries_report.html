💡 Executive Insights

  • Website enquiries remain the strongest source of new business.
  • Cardiff continues to lead the network for enquiry conversion.
  • Response times have improved by approximately 12%.
  • Recruitment demand continues to increase month-on-month.
  • Google Business Profile continues to generate high-quality leads.
  • Telephone enquiries convert at a higher-than-average rate.
  • Follow-up speed directly impacts conversion performance.
  • Most enquiries are received between 09:00 and 13:00.

🎯 Head Office Recommendations

  • Share Cardiff's enquiry handling process across the network.
  • Provide additional enquiry handling training where response times exceed two hours.
  • Increase marketing investment in lower-performing territories.
  • Introduce automated follow-up reminders for uncontacted enquiries.
  • Continue investment in SEO and Google Business Profile optimisation.
  • Review recruitment advertising performance monthly.
  • Monitor enquiry quality alongside enquiry volume.
  • Carry out quarterly franchise performance reviews.

📝 Executive Commentary

The Heritage Healthcare franchise network continues to demonstrate consistent enquiry growth, supported by strong digital marketing activity and improving response times across most territories. Cardiff and Bristol continue to outperform the network average, while several offices have opportunities to improve enquiry response times and conversion performance. Continued sharing of best practice and targeted operational support should further strengthen enquiry management across the network.
